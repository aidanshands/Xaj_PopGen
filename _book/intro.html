Genomic Insights into Walnut Blight: A Workbook

2 Introduction

The objective of this research is to characterize the population structure and prevalence of copper resistance of the walnut blight pathogen, Xanthomonas arboricola pv. juglandis ( Xaj ) collected in central and Northern California between the years of 1994 to 2019. Additionally, we are interested in elucidating the type III secretion system effector (T3E) diversity and assessing where the Californian Xaj population fits in a global context. We also sequenced 20 strains of Xanthomonas arboricola pv. pruni ( Xap ), the causal agent of bacterial spot of almond, and included those in some of the analyses.

2.1 Hypotheses

  • Genes associated with and/or responsible for copper resistance are prevalent in the CA Xanthomonas arboricola pv. juglandis population and correlate with the respective copper-sensitivity phenotype in vitro.
  • Xaj and Xap exhibit variation in type III secretion system effector repertoires.
  • CA Xaj strains form distinct genetic clusters when compared with other global strains.
  • The CA Xaj population does not encode any plasmids.
